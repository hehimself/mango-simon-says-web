MOVEAlready on the device

No animation downloads during play. A dedicated renderer keeps Mango moving at 24 fps.

Development measurements from five recorded commands on the 1.75C: 559 ms median / 628 ms worst to the first clip frame; 42 ms median from accepted decision to presentation. First frame is not the finished pose. Results vary with Wi-Fi, services, and speech; the 500 ms p95 target has not been reached.

2Install Mango

Connect with a USB data cable and close other serial monitors. Select the device when your browser asks.

No keys are included in the download. Preview release: the new USB setup has not yet been tested on hardware.

Device not appearing?

Try another data cable and close any app using the USB port. To enter download mode, hold BOOT while reconnecting USB, release it, then try again. Keep the cable connected until installation finishes.

3Add your settings

After installing, reconnect USB if asked. Already installed Mango? You can update settings here without reinstalling.

Settings go directly from this page to your device over USB. They are not uploaded or saved in browser storage. They remain on the device until erased or replaced. Anyone with physical flash access may recover them.
